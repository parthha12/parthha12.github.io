Biography

Parth Harish is a software engineer at RegKeyAI, building agents that draft FDA submissions, and an M.S. Data Science student at Harvard. Before that he was a machine learning engineer at SelectorAI and Amazon, working on network traffic models and LLM evaluation for Alexa.

He earned his B.S. in Computer Science and Statistics from Carnegie Mellon University and earlier interned at MIAX Exchange on high-throughput options market data. Outside of work, he is building Jot, a local-first AI secretary with multi-agent tool routing and proactive memory.
